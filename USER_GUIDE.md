## 1. What is Gubbi?

**Gubbi** is an interactive live quiz game platform. Think of it as a fun, vibrant gameshow where:
- **The Host (You)** creates or picks a quiz and displays questions on a big screen, classroom projector, or video call (Zoom/Teams/Google Meet).
- **The Players (Students or Audience)** join from their smartphones, tablets, or computers using a simple 6-digit PIN and answer questions in real time.
- **Everyone** enjoys friendly competition with lively music, instant feedback, streaks, and a 3D celebration podium at the end.

## 2. Step 1: Getting Started (Sign Up & Log In)

### How to Create a New Account (Sign Up)
1. Open the Gubbi homepage in your browser.
2. In the top-right corner of the screen, click the **Sign Up** or **Get Started Free** button.
3. Fill in your details:
   - **Your Name**: Enter your full name or title (e.g., *Prof. Davis* or *Sarah Jenkins*).
   - **Email Address**: Enter your work or personal email address.
   - **Password**: Create a secure password that you can remember.
4. Click **Create Account**. You will immediately be logged in and taken to your personal Host Dashboard!

### How to Log In to an Existing Account
1. From the homepage, click **Log In** in the top navigation bar.
2. Enter your registered email address and password.
3. Click **Log In** to access your dashboard.

> **Forgot your password?**  
> Click the **Forgot password?** link on the login screen, enter your email address, and you will receive a secure reset link to choose a new password.

## 3. Step 2: Exploring Your Dashboard

Once logged in, your **Dashboard** is your mission control center:

- **Quick Stats Bar**: See at a glance how many quizzes you have created, how many live sessions you have run, and how many players have participated.
- **Left Navigation Bar**:
  - **Dashboard**: Returns you to this home hub anytime.
  - **My Quizzes**: View, search, and manage all quizzes you have built or saved.
  - **Create Quiz**: Opens the quiz authoring studio.
  - **My Reports**: Access past session scores, participant rankings, and download reports.
  - **Settings / Profile**: Update your name, change your password, or manage preferences.
- **Quick-Start Curated Quizzes**: Don't have time to create a quiz right now? Explore pre-made trivia packs (Science, Pop Culture, World Geography, Coding) and launch a game with one click!

## 4. Step 3: Creating a Quiz

Click **+ Create Quiz** in the left sidebar or the purple **Create Quiz** button on your dashboard. You have two easy ways to build questions:

### Option A: Using the AI Quiz Assistant (Fastest & Easiest)
Let Gubbi's built-in AI build a complete quiz for you in seconds!

1. At the top of the Create Quiz screen, click the **AI Assistant** button.
2. An interactive popup window will appear:
   - **Prompt**: Type any topic you want (e.g., *"Solar system trivia for 5th graders"* or *"Python programming basics"*).
   - **Difficulty Dropdown**: Choose between **Easy** (casual fun), **Medium** (standard challenge), or **Hard** (deep mastery).
   - **Question Count Dropdown**: Pick how many questions you want (e.g., 3, 5, 8, 10, or 15 questions).
3. Click **Generate Quiz**.
4. The AI will instantly generate questions with 4 answer choices, correct answers marked, and timer limits suggested.
5. Review the questions on screen. You can uncheck any question you don't like, or click **Import All** to drop them straight into your quiz!

---

### Option B: Building Manually from Scratch
You can also type your own custom questions step-by-step:

1. **Quiz Details**:
   - **Quiz Title**: Give your quiz a clear name (e.g., *Biology Midterm Review*).
   - **Category**: Pick a category from the dropdown (Science, History, General Knowledge, etc.).
   - **School / Organization Name**: (Optional) Add your school or company name to brand the quiz.
2. **Adding Questions**:
   - Click the question card to type your **Question Text**.
   - Fill in up to **4 Answer Options**.
   - Click the **circle or checkbox** next to the correct answer choice so Gubbi knows which one is right.
   - **Question Timer**: Use the timer slider to choose how many seconds players have to answer (from 5 seconds up to 120 seconds; default is 20s).
   - Click **+ Add Another Question** to add more questions.

---

### Adding Media & Backgrounds
Make your quiz look stunning and visually engaging:
- **Pexels Photo Library**: Click **Pexels Library** to search through millions of royalty-free stock photos directly inside Gubbi. Click any picture to set it as a background.
- **Upload Your Own**: You can upload any image file from your computer.
- **Background Mode**: Toggle **"Same background for all questions"** to keep a uniform look, or turn it off to pick a unique photo for each individual question!

### Saving Your Quiz
- **Save as Draft**: Need to step away and finish later? Click *Save as Draft*. Your work is safely stored under your *Drafts* tab.
- **Save & Publish**: Ready to play? Click *Save & Publish*. The quiz is immediately available to host in live games!

## 5. Step 4: Managing Your Quizzes ("My Quizzes")

Click **My Quizzes** in the left sidebar to access your quiz collection:
- **Tabs**: Switch between **All Quizzes**, **Drafts** (in-progress), and **Published** (ready to host).
- **Search Bar**: Type any keyword to find a quiz in your collection instantly.
- **Card Actions**:
  - **Host / Play**: Launches a live multiplayer room immediately.
  - **Edit**: Re-opens the quiz studio to add, edit, or remove questions anytime.
  - **Duplicate**: Clones the quiz so you can make variations without changing the original.
  - **Delete**: Safely removes a quiz you no longer need.

## 6. Step 5: Hosting a Live Game Battle

When you are ready to play with your students or audience:

1. On any published quiz, click the green **Host Game** or **Play** button.
2. You will enter the **Host Lobby**:
   - A unique **6-Digit Game PIN** is displayed in bold letters (e.g., `482 910`).
   - A scannable **QR Code** is shown on screen.
3. **Display this screen to your audience** (put it on the classroom projector or share your screen on Zoom/Teams).
4. As players join, their chosen nicknames and funny avatars will appear in the lobby in real time.
5. Once everyone is in the room, click the big **Start Game** button!

## 7. Step 6: How Players Join & Play (Player View)

Players do **not** need to create an account or download any app!

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       PLAYER EXPERIENCE                     │
│                                                             │
│   1. Open browser on phone ➔ Go to the join link            │
│   2. Enter the 6-Digit PIN displayed on the host screen     │
│   3. Pick a Nickname and Avatar                             │
│   4. Watch the question on the Host screen                  │
│   5. Tap the matching colorful shape on their phone:        │
│          ▲ Red Triangle       ◆ Blue Diamond                │
│          ● Yellow Circle      ■ Green Square                │
│   6. See instant feedback, points, and rank!                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Gameplay Details:
- **Scoring**: Players earn points for answering correctly. The faster they submit their answer, the more bonus points they receive!
- **Streaks**: Answering multiple questions correctly in a row triggers an *Answer Streak* with bonus multipliers.
- **Between Questions**: The host screen reveals the correct answer and displays a live **Leaderboard** showing the top 5 players.
- **Grand Finale (The Podium)**: When the final question ends, Gubbi celebrates the top 3 champions with an animated 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place podium accompanied by confetti!

## 8. Step 7: Viewing Results & Downloading Reports

After a game concludes, all performance data is automatically saved:

1. Click **My Reports** in your sidebar.
2. You will see an analytics dashboard featuring:
   - **Total Quizzes Hosted**
   - **Total Participants**
   - **Average Accuracy Score** across all players
3. Click **View Report** on any past game session to open detailed analytics:
   - Complete player scoreboard (Rank, Correct Answers, Wrong Answers, Points).
   - "Fastest Solver" badges and hardest question highlights.
4. **Export Your Report**:
   Need to enter grades, submit attendance, or share results with school leadership? Click any of the export buttons:
   - **Excel (`.xlsx`)**: Formatted multi-tab spreadsheet for Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
   - **PDF Document (`.pdf`)**: A beautifully formatted, printable A4 document with letterhead and clean result tables.
   - **Word Document (`.docx`)**: Editable Microsoft Word report.
   - **CSV (`.csv`)**: Raw data ready to import directly into your school's LMS (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, Google Classroom).

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Do players need to sign up or download an app?
**No.** Players join instantly by opening any web browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge) on their phone, tablet, or laptop and entering the 6-digit game PIN.

### Can I edit a quiz after creating it?
**Yes.** Go to **My Quizzes**, find your quiz, and click **Edit**. You can update questions, change timers, replace background photos, and save your changes anytime.

### What happens if a player gets disconnected during a game?
If a player accidentally closes their browser tab or loses Wi-Fi temporarily, they can simply re-open the page and re-enter the game PIN to rejoin their active session.

### Can I share my quizzes with another teacher or coworker?
Yes! You can duplicate any quiz or use curated packs to share trivia content across classrooms and teams.

### How do I switch between Light and Dark mode?
Click the **Theme toggle icon** (sun/moon) in the navigation bar to switch between the crisp bright daylight view and the sleek dark mode anytime.
